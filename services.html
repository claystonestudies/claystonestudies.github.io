Life Coaching

Organization, well-being, and understanding are things that everyone regards as important. These three values are the framework of my life coaching approach. We can clarify the goals that you already have, or that you would like to develop, and then determine the plans for you to successfully approach and arrive there. From wherever you stand in life, with your obstacles and successes, there we can begin, working together to improve your path forward. You can get a sense of my style and approach through my videos on the Claystone Studies YouTube channel, linked on the About page.

A fundamental principle that I bring to the table, and which frames my life coaching sessions, is that well-being is to be promoted (i) for individuals, (ii) for communities, and most importantly, (iii) for the planet and the planetary community, since the total planetary community contains all other communities and individuals within itself, but no individual or community contains within itself the planetary community. In this way, I seek to contribute to the alignment of the individual with the whole, and this is connected with the values of organization, well-being, and understanding that frame my life coaching.

Like everyone else, I have strengths and shortcomings, and so my life coaching will also have some degree of errors and shortcomings, in addition to positive qualities.

In summary, my life coaching is oriented around six values.

  • Goals
  • Plans
  • Organization
  • Well-being
  • Understanding
  • Alignment with the whole

Life Coaching offers support for clarifying goals, developing plans, and promoting organization, well-being, and understanding. It is not medical, legal, financial, or crisis care. It is not for addressing an emergency or danger. No outcome in Life Coaching is guaranteed. Robert has strengths and shortcomings, and errors are part of his life coaching.
