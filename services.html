Life Coaching Session

1 hour on Zoom

Organization, fulfillment, and understanding are things that everyone regards as important. These three values are the framework of my life coaching approach. We can determine the goals that you already have, or that emerge intuitively from your values, and then identify plans for you to successfully approach and arrive there. From wherever you stand in life, with your obstacles and successes, there we can begin, working together to improve your path forward. You can get a sense of my style and approach through my videos on the Claystone Studies YouTube channel, linked on the About page.

A fundamental principle that I bring to the table, and which frames my Life Coaching sessions, is that harmony is to be promoted (i) for individuals, (ii) for communities, and most importantly, (iii) for the planet and the planetary community, since the planetary community is the ground and support for all communities and individuals on the planet.
